How this differs from the static comparison app

Model equations, reservoir construction, and the three neuron dynamics (homogeneous leaky-integrator, heterogeneous leaky-integrator / Tanaka et al. 2022 DTS-ESN, and rectify→resonate→delay band-pass pools / Wyffels et al. 2008, structurally motivated by Köndgen et al. 2008's finding that real cortical neurons behave as broadband 2-pole filters) are identical to the static app — see its footer for the full equations and honest caveats. The only difference here: after "Reset & Train" fits the readout for all three conditions once via teacher-forced ridge regression (a quick batch step), the autonomous free-run phase runs forever, one sample at a time, on a scrolling strip-chart, rather than being pre-computed in bulk and plotted as a finished curve. There is no fixed test-set length or repeat-averaging here — it's a single live trial, meant for watching divergence happen rather than for producing publication-quality averaged statistics (use the static app for that). Rolling NRMSE meters use a 150-sample trailing window normalized by a σ estimated once at training time.
