Hit Run, then Ignite to kick the network into the high-rate attractor. With low SFA it latches and the amber marker parks on the upper stable fixed point. Raise SFA b and the up fixed point slides left and annihilates against the saddle — the up-state collapses to down.
Model. Each neuron obeys τ_m dV/dt = −(V−E_L) + RI(t) with threshold V_θ=20, reset V_r=10, refractory t_rp=2 ms. Connectivity is sparse & random (fixed in-degree) — this decorrelates inputs and keeps the network in the asynchronous-irregular state where the Ricciardi/Siegert transfer function Φ(μ,σ) is valid. Recurrent excitation is low-pass filtered (slow τ_SE) — the standard ingredient that makes a high-rate attractor dynamically stable — while inhibition is fast. External input is an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck current; its injected amplitude is σ/√2 so the stationary membrane variance matches the σ²/2 assumed by Ricciardi. The right panel solves ν_out=Φ_E(μ_E(ν,ν_I(ν)),σ_E) live and marks the fixed points; adaptation w̄ shifts the curve and folds the up-state away (saddle-node).
